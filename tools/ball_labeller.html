How to

Seen: drag a tight box around the match ball. That answers the frame.
Hidden (H): in the picture but you can't see it (behind a player, under a foot). Then optionally click where you think it is.
Out (O): the match ball is not in the picture.
Unsure (U): you can't tell. That frame is not scored, so use it rather than guess.
Other ball: Shift + drag boxes a ball that is not the match ball (spare / warm-up balls). Any state.
Edit: click a box to select it, drag to move, drag a corner to resize, Del to delete.
Zoom: mouse wheel (the zoom is kept between frames). Pan: right-drag or Space+drag. F fit.
Every frame needs one answer. After H / O / U the next frame opens.
